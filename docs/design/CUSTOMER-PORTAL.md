## Guest intake and claim (AT-04)
Public catalog/version and eligibility -> party role/person or organization and representation -> vehicle facts as required -> service-specific data/documents -> itemized estimate/quote where available -> review terms/privacy and submit. Guest means no login, not exemption from legally required identity; email optional where service permits, no fabricated identifier. Server-approved intake context only, dealership referral validated; direct intake tenant routing proposed AT-04 D04 unresolved, do not assume arbitrary tenant. Rate limits, scoped opaque draft token, quarantine, idempotent submission, no public sequential ID. Receipt opaque reference, next steps, optional account creation; no public case detail from reference. No production processing until authorized active context and prerequisites. No forced marketing consent. Draft resume only approved secure mechanism, no local PII persistence. 
Vincular trámite: authenticated verified account, short-lived single-use token bound to specific case/party plus independent entitlement evidence; email/VIN/NIF/reference/payment alone insufficient. Show generic errors for invalid/expired/revoked/mismatch, request fresh link or manual review without existence leak. Proposed AT-04 parameters >=128-bit random, digest only, 30 minutes, 5 failed attempts remain pending approval; never hardcode UX as accepted. Operator verifies entitlement, atomic approval consumes token and creates explicit case grant; review exceeding expiry requires fresh token. Resend revokes old; concurrent/replay denied; disputed grant frozen only affected scope. No automatic all-customer grants or CRM merge. Company representative uses personal login, separate verified authority, scope/expiry and explicit grant; revocation immediate, prior lawful acts preserved. Manual specialist review for minors/estate/leasing/incapacity/corporate exceptions. Account registration alone grants nothing.

No automatic gallery save, remove location metadata from derivative, preserve original where required. Per-file progress/cancel/retry, idempotent finalize, scoped guest uploads, quarantine and malware/type/size checks before preview/OCR. Private storage ADR-005, short-lived scoped links, reauthorize each request, no public URL or shared cache. Unified public price ADR-007, no internal margin/credit exposure. No silent repricing or automatic payment. Consumer disclosure/withdrawal policy AT-20 pending.
Autorizaciones: principal, representative/professional, purpose/service, scope, validity, revocation request, evidence and status. Mandate separate from portal grant and professional entitlement; regulated acts only authorized gestoría, Ficha Reducida ordered by AutoTramita from qualified engineer in background. Signature inbox exact document/version/hash, signer role/authority, method and required assurance from approved service, preview all pages, explicit intent, consent/notice as applicable, provider handoff, pending/partial/completed/declined/expired/failed distinct. No blanket acceptance, no bulk signing, no OTP or drawn signature assumed legally sufficient; eIDAS level/provider/Spanish procedure requirements pending legal review. Validate evidence and signed artifact, preserve byte-for-byte original, audit, re-sign changed version. Signature completed not official filing or verified mandate automatically. Revocation blocks future dependent acts, notify review; past lawful acts preserved.
