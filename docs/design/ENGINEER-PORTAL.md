## Professional verification
Central reviewer verifies identity, qualification, competent professional scope, registration/Colegio where applicable, firm affiliation, evidence provenance, validity, contact, contractual/privacy terms and insurance where required. Do not assume all engineers require identical credentials or registration. Manual review, proposed draft/submitted/review/more evidence/verified/rejected/suspended/expired/revoked. Actual qualification and applicable Spanish procedure-specific requirements must be confirmed before production. No automatic registry verification assumed. Personal login; proposed MFA and step-up subject AT-02 approval; no private certificate keys/passwords/PIN collection. Recheck entitlement before signature and delivery, restrict affected grants immediately on suspension/revocation; preserve historical evidence and central reassignment. Separate technical authority from gestor representation and portal access. Engineer cannot file regulated administrative acts merely by being assigned.

## Procurement and assignment
AutoTramita creates technical order linked to authorized case and vehicle snapshot, service/version, scope, required deliverable, evidence checklist, contractual due date, fee version and responsible operator. Standalone Ficha service still case-linked; parent import case can depend on deliverable. Multiple technical orders per case only for distinct scopes or explicitly authorized revision; idempotency and duplicate review. Central eligibility matching by verified competence, scope, availability, conflicts, workload and contract; no automatic allocation assumed. Offer minimal non-identifying metadata, fee/scope/due, accept/decline reason, expiry, central reassignment. Atomic acceptance and optimistic version checks, one active responsible engineer per order unless approved collaboration; no subcontract or transfer without central approval. Accepted order grants minimum case/vehicle/document scope, revocable. No customer direct payment or engineer selection requirement. Manual handoff records manifest, recipient, channel, operator, timestamps and actual acceptance evidence; sent != accepted.

## Signature
Required signature type/format, certificate trust, signer eligibility, timestamps and any professional endorsement/visado determined by approved procedure and legal review, not assumed universal. Provider-neutral AT-09 workflow, PAdES candidate, no selected provider. Verify active grant, professional entitlement, complete required fields, resolved conflicts, correct template and exact draft hash before requesting signature. Signer previews exact bytes, explicitly consents and authenticates with approved provider or external signing process. Preserve signed original byte-for-byte, signature validation report, signer identity/certificate chain, integrity, revocation/time evidence, provider reference and timestamps. Pending/declined/expired/failed/unknown/valid distinct; no success from redirect or upload alone. Callback authentication/idempotency/reconciliation; no private keys. Changed draft requires new signature, preserve previous evidence. Revocation or invalid signature triggers review, no automatic conclusion about historical validity. No AutoTramita operator signs on engineer behalf.
## Quality and delivery
Proposed two layers: engineer self-check technical correctness and central operational quality review (identity match, template, completeness, signature evidence, deliverable scope); substantive technical second review only qualified reviewer where required, never unqualified backoffice certification. Review checklist and findings per exact signed version/hash, reviewer competence, decision, reasons and timestamps. Correction request structured, re-draft/re-sign where bytes change; old version superseded, not erased. No delivery while required checks unresolved. Gate at delivery: active authorized grant, current applicable entitlement, signed artifact validated as required, quality approval bound to same hash, correct order/vehicle/case, no dependent legal block. Central release to entitled parent case and authorized tenant/customer, signed original plus permitted evidence; short-lived download, audit, no public link. Delivery event recipient, version/hash, timestamp, channel, receipt/ack if available; sent != received != accepted by ITV. Parent case dependency marked satisfied only after authorized review, not automatically registered/completed. Rejected by ITV or later error opens correction/review linked to original, preserves prior outcome; no automatic resubmission. Revoke routine partner access after agreed window, retention pending AT-20.
