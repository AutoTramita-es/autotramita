Shared 256/72 sidebar, 64 topbar, 24px padding, 12 columns, Inter/Lucide, navy #082344, blue #005BCB, light/dark/system, accessible status labels, EUR es-ES, tabular numerals. Desktop tables and detail drawer; mobile cards and sticky authorized actions, 320px/200% zoom, WCAG 2.2 AA target. Tenant context server-authorized; branch filters for operations, company-wide credit only with company finance permission. Clear data on context switch. Loading, unavailable, stale, denied and true zero distinct. All examples fictional.
## 2. Financial overview
Snapshot cards: approved limit, reserved, utilized, usable credit, outstanding dealer obligations, overdue subset, pending collection (may overlap debt). Period cards: confirmed collections, refunds, issued invoices, rectifications; do not call collections revenue or profit. Explicit as-of timestamp and date basis; date filter affects flows not balances. Charts monthly confirmed inflows/refunds separately, due aging and credit composition, accessible table and drilldowns preserving scope. No cross-tenant totals. Company vs branch labels. Alerts for upcoming maturity, grace, suspension, reconciliation and disputes; no countdown until approved calendar. Quick actions quote, pay invoice, view credit, export, request refund only authorized.
## 3. AT-12 catalog and quote
Central versioned public retail tariff, same price for every dealer, service/version, effective dates, currency, tax presentation, statutory fees and taxes separate, conditional items and estimated vs confirmed amounts. Central draft/review/publish/retire; no dealer edit of public price; preserve historical snapshots. Public consumer total and tax disclosure subject to AT-20 approval. Quote wizard: select authorized branch/case/customer/payer; service version and eligibility; itemized service base, applicable IVA, statutory fee/tax lines, dealer-funded discount, total, expiry, payment choice; preview and explicit send/accept; draft/sent/accepted/expired/rejected/superseded/canceled illustrative. Changes after acceptance create revision and renewed consent when required; no silent repricing. Missing statutory amount marked estimate/pending, not zero; block final commitment if required. No arbitrary markup. Server validates decimal amounts, rounding and currency; integer minor units or decimal exact arithmetic, no float in authoritative ledger.
## 4. Discount and margin
Dealer funds discount entirely, AutoTramita agreed service amount unchanged. Discount permission separate from deferred payment; OWNER/ADMIN sets employee internal caps, server checks both cap and authorized scope. Proposed fixed EUR or percent on eligible service component only, no discount on statutory fees; caps and tax allocation pending AT-12/14/20. Display public price, discount, customer total, dealer-funded amount, agreed AutoTramita amount, dealer compensation/margin if authorized. Example exclusively illustrative gross commercial amounts: service 200, statutory fee 100, discount 20, customer total 280; assumed dealer compensation before discount 40, after discount 20, AutoTramita agreed amount 160 unchanged. This is not a tax invoice, settlement instruction or approved commission model; actual tax bases, IVA, commission, funding and negative margin policy unresolved. Margin report excludes pass-through taxes/fees, distinguishes gross contribution, costs, realized vs estimated; do not label net profit. Negative margin warning and block pending policy; no hidden fee to offset discount. Customer never sees internal margins.
## 5. AT-13 credit
One facility per legal tenant, shared across branches; only central AutoTramita sets limit, terms, cycle, grace/status. Show read-only terms and effective dates, request review not guaranteed. Proposed disjoint R reserved, U utilized, L limit; headroom max(0,L-R-U), usable zero when blocked; server authoritative, expose overlimit amount separately if L reduced below exposure. Receivables included in U where applicable, never subtract twice. Example L 20000 R 2000 U 8000 headroom 10000. Prepaid-only, active, credit suspended, unavailable; separate tenant suspension. Credit request: quote amount and actual eligible exposure calculated server-side (not necessarily customer total), preview obligation and maturity policy, explicit dealer acceptance, atomic check and reservation, idempotent request; concurrent branches cannot overspend. Reservation reference/case/amount/created/expiry/status; proposed reserve -> consume into utilized atomically (R decreases U increases), release unused/canceled/expired only when no committed obligation, partial consumption and release with audit. No automatic release on UI timeout; reconcile uncertain operations. Utilization and receivable allocation exact rules pending. Confirmed settlement reduces applicable exposure once; partial settlement allocated per approved policy; chargeback/refund reassess, no automatic credit increase from pending payment. Limit changes do not cancel existing obligations. Dealer owes AutoTramita regardless of customer collection; customer debt tracked separately and never double counted in facility. No consumer lending promise.
## 6. Deferred payment and maturity
Options prepaid and dealer-funded deferred only if authorized/approved; show responsible debtor, amount, terms, due date or pending calculation, customer payment status separately. Immediate/15/30/60 days examples, not defaults. Terms version pinned per obligation; invoice maturities never silently modified. Due table debtor type, invoice, branch attribution, original amount, outstanding, due date, overdue business days, collection status, dispute flag. ADR-007: unpaid after three applicable business days beyond due date suspends NEW credit only; prepaid remains, existing cases not automatically canceled. Calendar/timezone/cutoff and in-flight SEPA treatment unresolved; UI consumes authoritative state, no guessed calendar. Reactivate after confirmed settlement of all overdue obligations subject to other restrictions; no manual dealer override. Dunning reminders configurable proposal, no new legal deadline; disputes do not automatically pause obligation absent approved policy. Tenant suspension distinct (AT-03 decision 14).
## 7. AT-14 invoices and accounting
Separate customer invoice, dealer obligation/invoice, statutory receipt, payment receipt, quote and credit note/rectifying invoice; issuer/recipient and merchant-of-record unresolved. Do not issue duplicate tax invoices for same supply. Register reference/series, issuer, recipient, issue/tax date, due date, currency, net base, IVA rate/amount, statutory lines, total, outstanding, status, linked cases and payments. Tax rate and treatment configured by jurisdiction/service and reviewed; no blanket 21% or automatic exemption. Show statutory amounts separately, distinguish suplidos only if legal criteria met. Immutable issued invoice snapshot, corrections via legally appropriate rectification linked to original; no delete/edit issued invoice. Partial payments and allocations, outstanding vs disputed, credit note vs cash refund distinct. PDF secure per ADR-005; accounting export and invoice numbering/e-invoicing/VeriFactu requirements pending AT-14/20. Draft/issued/partially paid/paid/overdue/rectified illustrative dimensions not one enum. Payment receipt not tax invoice.
## 8. Card and SEPA
Central integrated PSP hosted checkout/tokenized fields; no PAN/CVV storage, no card data in logs. Customer or dealer payer explicitly selected, quote snapshot and exact amount, payment intent idempotency, provider authentication/SCA where applicable, redirect return pending until authenticated provider confirmation. Separate initiated/action required/authorized/captured/confirmed settlement/failed/canceled/expired/disputed/reversed states; mapping and point of confirmed payment per provider/accounting approval. No duplicate charge on retry, reconcile ambiguous timeout. SEPA authorized dealer collections only with verified mandate, scheme, notification, due date and revocation controls; pending not paid; returns reassess exposure. No assumption SEPA B2B vs Core, no storing bank credentials. Merchant-of-record, regulated funds flow, split settlement, provider, fees, payout and chargeback liability open; no internal wallet or custody promise. Show provider reference masked, amount, status, method, timestamps, allocation, reconciliation. Central authenticated webhook verification, replay protection, dedupe, out-of-order handling, reconciliation with provider statements and bank records; mismatch queue, no silent ledger edit.
## 9. Refunds and disputes
Request reason, original payment and invoice, eligible remaining refundable amount, line allocation, statutory fee recoverability review, authorization, preview effect on customer/dealer/credit and tax rectification. Partial/full, cumulative cap, idempotency, original method by default subject to provider/legal policy, no arbitrary third-party payout. Proposed request/review/approved/submitted/pending/confirmed/failed/rejected states; approval not payment. Central approval separation proposed, dealer requests only with permission. Chargeback separate dispute workflow, evidence, deadlines from provider, no double refund, confirmed reversal reopens/reassesses obligation according to approved rules. No automatic case cancellation or fee refund. Credit note and cash refund independently tracked. Audit and notification.
## 10. Reports and reconciliation
Reports by authorized tenant/branch, period, service, invoice, payment method, status: confirmed collections/refunds, outstanding/aging, credit movements, discounts, estimated/realized margin (only approved definitions), invoice register, settlement reconciliation. Aging proposed not due/1–30/31–60/61–90/90+ calendar days explicitly separate from grace business days. Cash vs accrual separate, currency and time basis, no double counting transfers or allocations. Export CSV/XLSX/PDF proposed, separate finance/bulk permissions, scoped short-lived downloads, CSV formula injection protection, audit and totals matching filters. Central reconciliation matches provider event, settlement batch, bank and ledger allocation; fees/payouts separate, unresolved differences queue, dual approval for adjustments proposed, append-only reversal/correction not overwrite. AT-19 definitions pending.
## 11. Permissions and security
Separate view company finance, branch finance, margin, quote, discount, deferred, pay, refund request, export, central credit admin, reconciliation and approval permissions. OWNER/ADMIN all branches subject to finance permission, MANAGER/EMPLOYEE assigned branches and explicit caps, VIEWER read-only authorized subset. Central-only credit terms/limits and public tariff publication; no dealer escalation. Customer sees own quote/invoice/payment only via entitlement, no internal margin/credit. Server RLS and composite tenant keys, every aggregate/export/job scoped, immutable audit with actor/tenant/branch/amount/currency/version/reason/outcome; no PAN/CVV/secret logs. Optional dealer MFA remains AT-03 decision 18; explicit confirmation for financial mutations, no new mandatory MFA policy. API idempotency and optimistic versioning ADR-004; financial ledger append-only and atomic transactions, reconciliation and audit. No financial action in mockup.
