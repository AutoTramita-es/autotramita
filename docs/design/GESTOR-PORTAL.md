Personal login, no shared credentials. Proposed states draft/submitted/under review/more information/verified/rejected/suspended/expired/revoked. Accept/decline with reason, expiry and reassign centrally; acceptance is not legal mandate or official filing. Proposed offer/accepted/declined/expired/reassigned/returned states, independent case workflow. No direct partner-to-partner transfer or subcontract without central approval and legal review. Phase 1 manual handoff remains backoffice-only.

## Deadlines
Legal vs internal dates separate, source/rule/calendar version, competent authority, timezone, days naturales/hábiles, confirmed vs provisional. Gestor cannot override statutory dates or centrally approved rules; propose factual correction and evidence. Internal task changes only explicit future permission, never legal dates. Existing 7/3/1/due/day-overdue notifications to authorized recipients; no backfill, dedupe, stop when resolved; partner routing proposed AT-16. AUTHORITY_REVIEW_REQUIRED daily reminder and 48h supervisor escalation; confirmed deadline <24h critical risk when authority review unresolved; uncertain dates urgent legal review, not fabricated. STATUTORY_DEADLINE_EXCEEDED review, no automatic irrecoverability; gestor supplies explanation/evidence, scoped OWNER/ADMIN/MANAGER or authorized central reviewer as existing rules, no unilateral clearance. LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED blocks only dependent actions; resolution re-evaluates without executing. Calendar/agenda and time filters, no browser legal calculations.

## Receipts and CSV
CSV = Código Seguro de Verificación, not spreadsheet. Preserve original bytes/hash, issuer, entry/reference, submission timestamp, procedure and applicable VIN/plate/applicant match; OCR extraction separate. Existing receipt workflow VERIFIED/PENDING_REVIEW/VERIFICATION_FAILED; evidence methods OFFICIALLY_VERIFIED vs MANUALLY_ACCEPTED distinct. Official source reachable and positive match plus case applicability required; outage stays PENDING_REVIEW, bounded backoff/rate limits, audit attempts; affirmative invalid/mismatch -> VERIFICATION_FAILED, manual review, no fraud accusation. Authorized correction of extracted CSV against unchanged original triggers fresh check, prior failure retained. No CSV alone not invalid; alternate evidence reviewed by authorized scoped supervisor per existing rules, not self-certified by gestor. DOCUMENT_CASE_MISMATCH blocks acceptance, missing data incomplete; POSSIBLE_DUPLICATE_RECEIPT review with privacy-safe cross-tenant matching, no other tenant disclosure; legitimate shared evidence requires scoped approval. Repeat official receipt validation only where supported, separate from deferred professional registry automation. Affirmative revocation -> RECEIPT_REVOKED, REVOKED_RECEIPT_REVIEW_REQUIRED for affected cases; closed cases POST_CLOSURE_REVIEW_REQUIRED without rewriting completion. Outage not revocation. No automatic deadline clearance, corrective filing or legal conclusions. Redact CSV from logs/notifications; source allowlist and safe outbound verification, no arbitrary URL fetch.
## Completion and handback

Return for review, central operator verifies completeness and applicable blockers, records completion per existing workflow. Official submission != official resolution != case completion. Rejected/partial outcomes, withdrawal/correction requests routed for review, no automatic refund or ownership update. Preserve original outcome; post-closure review separate. Revoke routine access after agreed handback/retention window (duration pending), retain only legally authorized audit/financial projection. Customer/tenant receive sanitized updates via AT-16, not internal partner notes.
