## Dealership page
H1 “Gestiona los trámites de tu concesionario desde un solo lugar”. Lead “Organiza vehículos, clientes, expedientes y documentación de tus centros de trabajo.” CTA “Registrar mi empresa”; secondary “Solicitar información”.
Sections in order: hero; six benefit cards (Expedientes centralizados, Clientes y vehículos, Gestión por centros, Equipo y permisos, Documentación y seguimiento, Control de costes); product workspace preview clearly labeled concept until real screenshot exists; published services; commercial model; onboarding; FAQ; final CTA.
Business model: one legal entity per tenant, branches share contractual responsibility and credit; global login may have separate memberships. OWNER/ADMIN all branches, others assigned branches and permissions. Discounts funded by dealership, public base price unchanged; statutory charges separate. Central card collection planned subject to provider approval. Prepaid access for new dealerships possible; credit only after separate approval by AutoTramita, no guaranteed amount or terms. Approved dealership owes AutoTramita regardless of customer collection. Overdue credit restriction distinct from tenant suspension: after three applicable business days beyond due date new credit usage suspended, prepaid access remains under ADR-007. Do not market credit as consumer lending. No invented subscriptions, commissions or fees. FAQ: “¿Puedo gestionar varios centros?” yes same legal entity; “¿Puedo ofrecer descuentos?” subject to permissions, dealership funded; “¿Tengo crédito al registrarme?” no automatic credit; “¿Cuándo puedo empezar?” after verification and approval.

## Onboarding UX
Separate low-data contact form (name, business email, organization, audience, optional phone/message, privacy notice; no identity documents) from formal application. Contact submission not activation. Formal dealership/importer flow:
1 “Tipo de empresa”: company/autónomo; intended activity; verified global account/contact per AT-02; existing account sign-in; no duplicate user requirement.
2 “Datos de la empresa”: legal name, legal form if applicable, NIF/NIE as applicable, registered/billing address, business contacts; server validates and checks duplicates. No disclosure of existing tenant staff; controlled access request/invitation or support for disputed representation.
3 “Representante”: identity and authority evidence, applicant OWNER candidate only; company registration/authority/identity, autónomo identity and tax/business registration evidence per AT-03. Exact document matrix legally reviewed. 
4 “Centro principal”: proposed BR-0001 with registered address prefilled, confirm actual address; activation only after tenant approval/address verification. Additional branches later under AT-03.
5 “Documentación y revisión”: private authorized upload, file validation/scan, progress/retry, review and correction, terms/privacy notices, separate optional marketing consent. No documents by public email or shared cache.
6 “Enviar solicitud”: idempotent submission, receipt and secure application access. “Solicitud recibida. Revisaremos tus datos y te avisaremos si necesitamos información adicional.” No production case access or active OWNER until manual approval.
Professional collaborator application branches after audience choice: specialty, legal identity/contact, professional registration/qualification and scope evidence as applicable, secure upload, manual review, agreement and scoped activation. Exact evidence, states and role model pending AT-10/11; no automatic tenant/credit creation.
Support resume, back, safe retry and supplement requests using verified scoped access; no sensitive localStorage/URL/analytics. Manual entry by AutoTramita follows same verification. No mandatory dealership 2FA contrary to AT-03 decision 18; optional protection, email verification and secure session rules. 
Tenant lifecycle DRAFT/PENDING_VERIFICATION/ACTIVE/SUSPENDED/CLOSED; verification separately PENDING/MORE_INFO_REQUIRED/APPROVED/REJECTED. UI labels “Borrador”, “En revisión”, “Información adicional requerida”, “Aprobada”, “No aprobada”; approval vs activation distinct. Rejection explanation and new review request when permitted, history retained. Credit application separate after business review, never a condition for prepaid access solely because credit is declined. No exact approval SLA.
