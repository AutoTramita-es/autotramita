## Login
Email and password if supported by selected identity provider; show/hide password, password manager and paste allowed, autocomplete, link to recovery, registration and professional onboarding. Provider-hosted equivalent acceptable; never collect provider credentials outside approved flow. Generic invalid credentials, throttled retry, expired session, unverified email and restricted account states without disclosing account existence to unauthenticated users. After authentication server resolves available authorized contexts: personal cases, dealership memberships/branches, partner or admin only if separately authorized. If multiple contexts show selector; no role elevation via UI or client identifiers. Safe allowlisted return destinations only; no open redirects. Optional 2FA for all dealership roles per AT-03 decision 18; other role policies pending AT-02. Session revocation and context isolation per AT-03. No social login promise.

## Private registration and case claim
Minimal email, password if provider supports, name only when needed; terms acknowledgment where applicable and privacy notice, separate optional unchecked marketing consent. No mandatory DNI/NIE, vehicle or document upload just to create global login. Verify email with scoped expiring single-use token; resend throttled, generic responses, invalid/expired/reused link recovery. Existing user sign-in path without enumeration. Registration does not create tenant membership, prove vehicle ownership or grant access to cases. Guest service submission remains available. Link anonymous case only after short-lived single-use proof of control/entitlement beyond email matching; server validates each case and tenant, audits claim, handles disputed claims via verified support. No public case lookup by plate/reference alone. Employee invitation flow verifies intended email, expiry (168h per AT-03), role/branch display before acceptance and server recheck. No automatic customer merge. 

## Recovery
Email entry, generic confirmation “Si existe una cuenta asociada, recibirás instrucciones para recuperar el acceso.” Rate limit and abuse controls. Reset via scoped single-use expiring token, no token in analytics/referrers/logs; invalid/expired/reused state offers fresh request. New password and confirmation, provider policy guidance, allow paste, success returns to login; revoke affected sessions according to AT-02 policy, notify account holder, audit without secrets. Lost email/MFA recovery through verified support, no security-question shortcut or manual password disclosure. Exact TTL, password policy and recovery evidence pending security review. No automatic access to business membership through recovery.

## Contact and help
Contact page: verified business channels only, no invented address/phone/hours; name, email, optional phone, topic (Servicios, Empresas, Soporte técnico, Facturación, Privacidad, Otros), message, privacy notice, submit. No public attachments, passwords, identity numbers or bank/card data; prompt to use authenticated secure channel for case-specific details. Rate limiting, accessible anti-abuse, success reference, failure retry, no promised SLA. Public contact does not authorize access to case. Help hub searchable published articles by trámites, documentación, cuenta, pagos, empresas, privacidad; accessible FAQ accordion, empty search suggestions, article feedback optional. Case-specific support after entitlement verification, secure ticket/thread and attachments only if authorized under ADR-005. Support access defaults to technical diagnostics; business data requires OWNER/ADMIN approval and time-limited scope, exceptional privileged access justified and audited per AT-03 decision 20. No impersonation or asking passwords. Support ticket model, routing and SLA pending domain review. Privacy requests separate protected workflow with proportionate identity verification, no public disclosure. 
FAQ copy: “¿Necesito una cuenta?” — “Puedes iniciar una solicitud sin registrarte.” “¿Cómo recupero mi acceso?” — “Utiliza la opción de recuperación.” “¿Cómo consulto mi expediente?” — “Accede y vincula tu solicitud mediante el procedimiento seguro.” “¿Puedo enviar documentos por el formulario de contacto?” — “Utiliza el canal seguro habilitado para tu expediente.” 

## Legal information — publication gate
These are content outlines, not approved legal texts or legal advice. Spanish legal review required before launch (GDPR, LOPDGDD, LSSI-CE, consumer rules and applicable professional regulation).
Aviso legal: verified operator legal name, NIF, registered address, registry details, contact, applicable professional authorization and regulator where required, IP and responsibility; distinguish platform from authorized external professionals and government agencies.
Privacidad: controller and contact/DPO if applicable, purposes and legal bases by activity, recipients/processors and role allocation with dealerships/partners, international transfers/safeguards, retention by category, rights and complaint to competent authority, mandatory vs optional data, automated decisions if applicable, source of data and minors policy if relevant. No invented retention or blanket consent. 
Cookies: actual inventory (provider, purpose, duration, first/third party), necessary vs optional categories, consent mechanism and withdrawal. Banner Spanish “Aceptar todas”, “Rechazar todas”, “Configurar” with equal prominence; no optional cookies before consent, no preselected optional categories, granular preferences, persistent footer link, versioned consent record and withdrawal as easy as acceptance. Necessary storage explained, not misclassified. 
Condiciones: distinguish B2C and B2B, operator and contracting party, service scope/eligibility, total tax-inclusive consumer price where determinable and separate statutory charges, payment and execution conditions, cancellation/refunds, applicable withdrawal rights and express requests/acknowledgments for early performance when legally required, complaints and dispute routes, external professional role, physical steps, liability subject to mandatory rights. B2B credit terms separately approved; no automatic credit or guarantees. No obsolete dispute links or unverified clauses. Checkout must show applicable terms version and clear payment obligation before binding order; durable confirmation and audit. Legal pages public, accessible, versioned with effective date; material changes handled according to legal basis/contract, not blanket forced consent.

## States and security
Loading, success, generic failure, validation, offline, throttled, expired link, restricted access, empty search. Accessible live status, field error associations, focus management, no sensitive values in logs/URLs/localStorage/shared cache; private uploads scoped and audited. Server authorization and RLS remain authoritative. SEO public help/legal pages only where published; no indexing tokens or private content. Use existing logo/pattern assets; raster images optional and pending approval.
